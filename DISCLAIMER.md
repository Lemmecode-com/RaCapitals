## 3. Indicative Prices & Liquidity Risk
* **Non-Binding Quotes:** Prices displayed on this portal are **purely indicative** and derived from private secondary market enquiries, historical transaction records, and internal research assessments. They do not represent live quotes from any recognized stock exchange (such as NSE or BSE).
* **Price Volatility:** Actual transaction prices may differ materially based on deal size, lot size, stamp duty, counterparty availability, market sentiment, and bilateral negotiation.
* **Severe Illiquidity Risk:** Unlisted and pre-IPO shares are inherently illiquid. Unlike exchange-traded equities, there is no guaranteed secondary market, fixed bid-ask spread, or assured exit window. Investors may not be able to sell their shares when desired, or may be forced to accept a significant discount.
