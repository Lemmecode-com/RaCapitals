## 1. Introduction & Scope
Taurus Magnus ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and the **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011**.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, disclose, and protect personal data when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, or communicate with our team via WhatsApp, email, or telephone.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect
We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiries and facilitate off-market communication:
1. **Contact Information:** Full name, email address, mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact number, city and state of residence.
2. **Enquiry Details:** Company name of interest, approximate lot size or transaction volume, investment preferences, and message content submitted through our contact or enquiry forms.
3. **Technical & Browsing Data:** Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device identifier, operating system, pages visited, visit timestamps, and referring URLs.
4. **KYC Verification Data (if entering into a bilateral transaction):** PAN card copy, Aadhaar/address proof, Client Master List (CML) / Demat Account details (CMR), and bank account verification details required strictly for statutory compliance under Indian anti-money laundering (PMLA) regulations and off-market transfer norms.

---

## 3. Purpose of Processing Personal Data
We process your personal data strictly for specified, lawful purposes:
* To respond to your enquiries regarding indicative prices, share availability, and transaction mechanics.
* To communicate market insights, research updates, or DRHP news if you have explicitly opted in.
* To verify user identity and prevent fraud, money laundering, and unauthorized transactions.
* To comply with statutory legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory reporting obligations under Indian law.
* To analyze website performance, improve user interface, and maintain server security.

---

## 4. Consent and Lawful Basis
By submitting your contact details on our website or sending an enquiry via WhatsApp/email, you grant **free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous consent** to Taurus Magnus to process your data for the stated enquiry and communication purposes in accordance with Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer.

---

## 5. Non-Sale Policy & Data Confidentiality
* **We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data** to any third-party marketing companies, telemarketers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.
* Access to personal information is strictly restricted to authorized personnel who require it to serve your requests.

---

## 6. Disclosure of Personal Data
We may disclose your personal data only under the following limited and lawful circumstances:
1. **Authorized Service Providers:** With cloud infrastructure providers (secure hosting), SMS/WhatsApp business API providers, and email delivery platforms bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
2. **Transaction Counterparties & Depository Participants:** When you proceed with a verified transfer of shares, your demat details (Client Master List) are shared with the designated Depository Participant (CDSL / NSDL) and clearing banks solely to complete off-market DIS share delivery.
3. **Legal & Regulatory Authorities:** When mandated by law, court order, or written summons issued by SEBI, RBI, Income Tax Department, Enforcement Directorate (ED), or law enforcement agencies.

---

## 7. Data Security and Storage
We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure:
* Encryption in transit using modern **Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL)**.
* Role-based access controls and secure database authentication.
* Data is hosted and processed on secure cloud servers complying with Indian data residency norms.

---

## 8. Data Retention Period
* General contact enquiries without subsequent transactions are retained for a period of up to twenty-four (24) months, after which they are securely deleted or anonymized.
* Transactional and KYC records associated with off-market share transfers are retained for a minimum period of eight (8) years to comply with statutory mandates under the Companies Act, 2013 and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA).

---

## 9. Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023
As a data principal under Indian law, you have the following enforceable rights:
1. **Right to Access Information:** Request a summary of your personal data processed by us and the processing activities undertaken.
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, or erasure of data no longer required for statutory purposes.
3. **Right to Grievance Redressal:** Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer regarding any perceived violation of your data privacy.
4. **Right to Nominate:** Nominate an individual who may exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

---

## 10. Cookies Policy
Our website uses minimal, essential functional cookies necessary for page navigation, session management, and anonymous traffic aggregation. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies or third-party behavioral profiling trackers.
